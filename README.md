# Applying for Benefits &amp; Assistance: A Beginner's Manual

Plain-English, step-by-step help for finding benefit programs, gathering documents, filling out forms, and tracking applications &mdash; written so paperwork feels understandable, not intimidating.

## What this is

A beginner-friendly manual and static HTML site that walks you through the life-admin of applying for benefits and assistance:

- **Finding programs** you might qualify for (food, healthcare, housing, income, and more)
- **Gathering documents** (ID, proof of income, proof of address, and where to get missing paperwork)
- **Filling out forms** without getting overwhelmed &mdash; section by section
- **Tracking applications** and following up while you wait for a decision
- **Printable checklists** and **what to expect** timelines

## Who it's for

Anyone who feels overwhelmed by benefits paperwork &mdash; especially beginners, caregivers, and people navigating life changes. Written in plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## How to use it

### Read the manual
The Markdown source lives in `docs/`:

- `docs/index.md` &mdash; overview and how to get started
- `docs/finding-programs.md` &mdash; chapter 1: finding programs
- `docs/gathering-documents.md` &mdash; chapter 2: gathering documents
- `docs/filling-forms.md` &mdash; chapter 3: filling out forms
- `docs/tracking-applications.md` &mdash; chapter 4: tracking applications
- `docs/checklists.md` &mdash; printable checklists
- `docs/what-to-expect.md` &mdash; timelines, outcomes, next steps

### Browse the static site
The `site/` folder is a complete static website &mdash; no build step, no internet required, no dependencies. Open any `.html` file in your browser:

- `site/index.html` &mdash; home
- `site/finding-programs.html`
- `site/gathering-documents.html`
- `site/filling-forms.html`
- `site/tracking-applications.html`
- `site/checklists.html`
- `site/what-to-expect.html`

The checklists page is interactive in the browser: check items off and they strike through.

## Disclaimer: education only, not legal advice

This guide is educational information only. It is **not legal advice**, benefits counseling, or a substitute for talking with a qualified professional.

Program rules, eligibility requirements, application steps, and deadlines vary by state, county, city, and program &mdash; and they change over time. Always confirm current requirements with the program or agency directly before applying.

## Your rights

- You have the right to apply for benefits you may qualify for.
- You have the right to be treated with respect by agency staff.
- You have the right to ask questions and get help understanding forms.
- You have the right to appeal a decision if your application is denied.
